A different kind of vet practice

Independent by choice. Personal by nature.

Vets on the Common was created in 2021 by Dr Ellie and Dr Michael after years working in charity and private veterinary clinics.

They built an independent practice around communication, honesty and trust. As full-time vets who also manage the clinic, they keep the focus on the individual needs of pets and their people.

That means a welcoming team, continuity of care and time to talk through the decisions that matter to you.

A little more room to relax

A calmer corner of the clinic, made for cats.
