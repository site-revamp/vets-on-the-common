# Vets on the Common – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Vets on the Common is an independent veterinary practice established in 2021, serving pet owners in Clapham, London. Founded by veterinary professionals Dr Ellie and Dr Michael, the practice provides personalised veterinary care for dogs, cats, rabbits, birds and other small animals.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the practice's independent identity, highlighting its key differentiators and making it easier for local pet owners to understand services, compare pricing and arrange appointments.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Presentation of the Practice's Differentiators
- Created a more focused homepage introduction highlighting independent ownership and personalised veterinary care.
- Brought key selling points forward, including consultations lasting at least 20 minutes, dedicated cat facilities and in-house diagnostic services.
- Improved the visibility of the reasons pet owners might choose the practice.

**Problem addressed:** Important differentiators were previously spread across different sections, making the practice's unique value less immediately apparent.

### 2. Clearer Appointment and Contact Journey
- Increased the prominence of the Book an Appointment and Call the Practice actions.
- Improved the registration pathway for new clients.
- Made emergency and out-of-hours information, including the Wolfe Vets Fulham contact details, easier to locate.
- Added more accessible booking actions throughout the homepage.

**Problem addressed:** Visitors lacked a sufficiently clear route to booking, contacting the practice or finding urgent veterinary assistance.

### 3. Improved Pricing and Service Information
- Introduced greater visibility for common veterinary service prices.
- Added a clearer route to the complete pricing information.
- Improved the presentation of what is included in common treatments.
- Clarified that complex procedures may require individual estimates.

**Problem addressed:** Visitors had limited access to an at-a-glance overview of treatment costs, making price comparisons and booking decisions more difficult.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Developed a more welcoming and professional appearance reflecting the practice's independent, relationship-led approach.
- **Homepage Layout:** Improved content hierarchy by prioritising the clinic's differentiators, appointment actions, services and pricing.
- **Service Presentation:** Made veterinary services and facilities easier to discover and understand.
- **User Experience:** Simplified access to registration, appointment booking, telephone contact and emergency information.
- **Content Consistency:** Strengthened the messaging around personalised care, longer consultations and the practice's independent identity.
- **Overall Appearance:** Created a more focused, informative and accessible homepage for local pet owners.

---

**Project Focus:** Stronger independent practice positioning, a simpler appointment journey and greater pricing transparency.
